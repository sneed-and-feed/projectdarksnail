## 1. Backbone Structural Mapping: `Meta-Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct` vs. `google/gemma-4-31B`

## 2. Seven Deep Audit Bugs Identified & Fixed

1. **Infinite Recursion & Ephemeral Path in `_get_dynamic_topology_router()`**:
   - *Bug*: `_get_dynamic_topology_router()` previously called itself in its `try:` block before falling back to a hardcoded path.
   - *Fix*: Replaced with `_get_topology_module()` that searches `[here.parent, here, Path.cwd()] + [Path(sp) for sp in sys.path]` and loads `llama_surgery/topology.py` directly via `importlib.util.spec_from_file_location` without triggering `llama_surgery/__init__.py` (which eagerly imports `transformers.Trainer`).
2. **3D `(B, S, head_dim)` `position_embeddings` Slicing & Decode `position_offset` in `apply_gemma4_prope`**:
   - *Bug*: HuggingFace's `Gemma4TextRotaryEmbedding.forward` returns 3D `(batch_size, seq_len, head_dim)` `cos/sin` tensors (`modeling_gemma4.py` lines 2064-2090). `cos[:S]` previously sliced along `dim=0` (`batch_size`) instead of `dim=1` (`seq_len`), and the internal 2D `_rope_cache` fallback started at position `0` during single-token autoregressive decode (`seq_len == 1, past_len > 0`).
   - *Fix*: `apply_gemma4_prope` now slices `cos[:, :S, :]` on 3D tensors and indexes `cos[position_offset : position_offset + S]` (or `cos[position_ids]`) on 2D caches.
3. **Zero-Shot `init_mode="collapse"` Producing `100%` Dense Masks Without a Gemma 4 Sidecar**:
   - *Bug*: Because `llama31_8b_router_v3.safetensors` (`32` layers, `in_dim=256`) cannot load into `SurgicalGemma4Attention` (`10` Global layers, `in_dim=768` on `q_nope + k_nope`), initializing routers with `init_mode="collapse"` (`bias = [5.0, -5.0]`) sends all tokens to branch `0` when no Gemma 4 sidecar exists, collapsing `v4_triad@r` and `darksnail@r` into `100%` dense attention.
   - *Fix*: Added `router_init_mode="haar_dct"` (`_init_router_haar_dct_spectral_hash`), which initializes `DynamicTopologyRouter` with a deterministic orthonormal Haar-DCT spectral hyperplane basis on `q_nope + k_nope` for genuine zero-shot hierarchical tree sparsity, plus `warmup_gemma4_routers_kl(...)` for in-notebook self-supervised Block-KL + Dual Lagrangian router warmup.
4. **Autoregressive Decode (`seq_len == 1, L_kv > 1`), `window@r` / `oracle@r` Overrides, and Measure-Preserving Donor Pruning**:
   - *Bug*: During `model.generate(...)` (`seq_len == 1, L_kv > 1`), `_compute_qk_routing` only routed the single new token (`assignments` length `1`) while `branch_id` needed `L_kv` past token branches; live decode-step neighborhood shielding (`decode_k=64`) and `surgical_mask_override in ("window", "oracle")` were also missing.
   - *Fix*: Added full prefill-to-decode `_cached_assignments` / `_cached_block_assignments` tail-block updating, live decode-step `max_pool1d` neighborhood span shielding (`decode_k=64`), measure-preserving prefill donor pruning (`allowed_causal(darksnail@r) <= allowed_causal(v4_triad@r)`), `_block_list_prefill` + `_apply_darksnail_block_bridge`, and `window@r` / `oracle@r` overrides.
5. **Inline `DarkSnailGemma4GlobalHook` Gating, Zero-Allocation Patching (`skip_proj_init=True`), & 4-bit `bitsandbytes` Compatibility**:
   - *Bug*: When `surgical_enable_global_hook=True`, `k_pruned = k * keep_mask` zeroed out keys even during `dense` (`req_depth == 0`) and `window@r` runs; `SurgicalGemma4Attention.__init__` allocated ~7.5 GB of temporary FP32 linear projections before overwriting them; and reading `old_attn.k_proj.weight.shape` under `bitsandbytes` NF4 (`Params4bit` shape `(N//2, 1)`) miscomputed `num_kv_heads`.
   - *Fix*: Gated inline KV pruning off during `req_depth == 0` and `override == "window"`, added `skip_proj_init=True` in `patch_gemma4_model`, and resolved `in_features` / `out_features` before `.weight.shape`.
6. **HuggingFace 5.5 KV Sharing (`past_key_values.shared_layers`) & Multimodal `text_config`**:
   - *Bug*: In HF `modeling_gemma4.py` (lines 2212-2239), KV sharing uses `past_key_values.shared_layers[self.kv_shared_layer_index]`, and `Gemma4ForConditionalGeneration` nests `hidden_size=5376` inside `model.config.text_config`.
   - *Fix*: Supported both `past_key_values.shared_layers[kv_shared_layer_index]` and legacy `shared_kv_states` dict, and resolved `getattr(model.config, "text_config", model.config)` across all patchers and hooks.
7. **Gemma 4 IT Thinking Mode (`<|channel>thought...<channel|>`) & List `eos_token_id = [1, 106]` in `darksnail/longbench.py`**:
   - *Bug*: `google/gemma-4-31B-it` enables `<|channel>thought\n...\n<channel|>` reasoning blocks by default and sets `eos_token_id = [1, 106, 50]`. On QASPER (`max_new_tokens=64`), an unstripped thought block triggers `_FirstLineStop` on its first internal newline or consumes all 64 tokens before answering.
   - *Fix*: Passed `enable_thinking=False` in `build_qasper_prompt_ids`, updated `_FirstLineStop` to ignore newlines inside open `<|channel>thought` blocks, added `strip_thinking_trace` before `score_qasper_f1`, and added `_resolve_pad_token_id(tokenizer)`.
